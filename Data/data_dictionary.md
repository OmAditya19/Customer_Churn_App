# **Understanding Data is Important!**

## Let’s see the variables(features) in the dataset.


---


**ID**: Shows a unique identification of a customer.


---


**Gender**: Biological sex (gender) of a customer. In this dataset, there are only 2 different options.

0: male

1: female


---


**Age**: The age of the customer in years, calculated as current year minus the year of birth of the customer at the time of the creation of the dataset

18 Min value (the lowest age observed in the dataset)

65 Max value (the highest age observed in the dataset)


---


**Tenure**: Number of months the customer has been with the company

1 Min value (the lowest tenure observed in the dataset)

60 Max value (the highest tenure observed in the dataset)


---



**Usage Frequency**: Frequency of service usage (monthly interactions)

1 Min value (the lowest Usage Frequency observed in the dataset)

30 Max value (the highest Usage Frequency observed in the dataset)


---



**Support Calls**: Number of calls to customer support.

0 Min value (the lowest number of support calls as observed in the dataset)

10 Max value (the highest number of support calls as observed in the dataset)


---



**Payment Delay**: Delay in payment (in days)

0 Min value (the lowest number of days with delayed payment as observed in the dataset)

30 Max value (the highest number of days with delayed payment as observed in the dataset)


---

**Subscription Type**: Type of subscription plan


1.   Basic      10$

2.   Standard   50$

3.   Premium    100$


---

**Contract Length**: Duration of the subscription contract

1.   Monthly --> 30
2.   Quartely --> 90
3.   Annually --> 365

---

**Total Spend**: Total amount spent by the customer

100$ Min Value

1000$ Max Value

---

**Last Interaction**: Number of days since the last customer interaction

1 Min Value (the least number of days since customer interaction)

30 Max Value (the highest number of days since customer interaction)

---

**Churn**: Whether the customer churned(left the service)

1 = YES

0 = NO
